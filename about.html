The Protocol To Give Voice To Planetary Intelligence.

The Protocol

Each event transforms atmospheric data into a spatial sonic environment.

Step 01

Simulate the Earth

Approximate Navier-Stokes equations. Discretize the atmospheric flow. Utilize the High Performance Infrastructures. Analyze the atmospheric dust data.

Step 02

Transform to Sound

Mineral composition of dust modulates the absorption of solar energy. It can warm or cool the atmosphere. Transform these oscillations into sound.

Step 03

Compose an Opera

Find harmonies and melodies in the sound sequence. Arrange them to tell a story of the processes from the tiniest particles to large scale atmospheric waves spreading over thousands of kilometers.

Step 04

Give Voice to the Planet

Understand the message beyond the calculations. Channel the state of the planet to the audience. Bring the voice of the planet closer to every individual present.
