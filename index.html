Electronic Solar Punk Opera

The Dust Protocol

State-of-the art atmospheric and climate modeling data turned into music.

Inspiration

Science

Dust particles absorb or scatter solar energy, warm or cool the air, seed clouds, fertilize oceans.
We simulate these processes on the most powerful supercomputers.
The Dust Protocol turns that data into music.

Music

Soundscape

Mineral composition becomes timbre.
Radiative oscillations become rhythm.
Atmospheric waves spreading over thousands of kilometres become the arc of a performance.
